# ✨ Jynox

### Your Mac. Your Music. Your Atmosphere.

**Jynox** is a premium macOS ambient lighting application that brings your display to life with beautiful RGB effects, music-reactive animations, and customizable glowing screen borders.

**Feel the Sound. See the Light.**

---

## Features

- 🎵 **Music Sync** — Dynamic RGB lighting that responds to your music.
- 🌈 **Ambient Lighting** — Beautiful glowing effects around your display.
- ✨ **Lighting Modes** — Lumn Wash, Plasma Silk, Corner Halo, and Screen Sync.
- 🎨 **Custom Palettes** — Jynox Rose, Sunset Vinyl, Ultraviolet, Emerald Zen, and more.
- 🎚️ **Advanced Controls** — Adjust glow intensity, border thickness, animation speed, and audio sensitivity.
- 💻 **MacBook Integration** — Display-aware lighting with notch contour controls.
- ⚡ **Smooth Visuals** — Anti-flicker smoothing and fluid animations.

## Installation

1. Download the latest `Jynox.dmg` from GitHub Releases.
2. Open the downloaded DMG file.
3. Drag `Jynox.app` into your Applications folder.
4. Launch Jynox.
5. Configure your preferred lighting effects.
